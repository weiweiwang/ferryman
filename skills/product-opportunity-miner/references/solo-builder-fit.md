# Solo-Builder Fit

Use this reference when the user wants opportunities suitable for an individual developer, a one-month MVP, SEO-led acquisition, low-touch sales, or indie hacking.

## Default Constraint

Assume the product should be feasible for one developer to prototype, launch, and validate in about one month unless the user says otherwise.

Prefer opportunities where V1 can be:

- A focused web tool, calculator, analyzer, tracker, checklist, planner, converter, generator, browser extension, or narrow dashboard.
- Useful with manual import, copy/paste, CSV upload, browser local storage, screenshots, or one simple integration.
- Discoverable through SEO pages, ASO, Reddit/community posts, directories, templates, examples, or shareable outputs.
- Safe to try without replacing a mission-critical system.

## Strong Solo-Builder Signals

- Users ask for a simple tool, not a platform.
- Current workaround is a spreadsheet, note, calendar, screenshot folder, manual checklist, copy/paste routine, or repeated prompt.
- The workflow has a clear input and output.
- The user can get value before connecting accounts or inviting a team.
- A static landing page can test intent with search traffic, examples, templates, or a free mini-tool.
- Support burden is low because users can self-serve and mistakes are reversible.

## Downgrade Signals

- Requires payments, invoices, SMS, email deliverability, calendar sync, bank/accounting sync, health data, legal data, or multiple SaaS integrations on day one.
- Requires trust transfer from an incumbent such as Jobber, Square, HoneyBook, QuickBooks, Notion, HubSpot, Salesforce, ServiceTitan, or Housecall Pro.
- Requires sales calls, migration, setup consulting, training, or customer support to become useful.
- Needs marketplace liquidity, user-generated supply, or local network effects.
- Bugs can directly cost users money, clients, appointments, compliance status, or health outcomes.
- Incumbents already solve the exact narrow job with a free or cheap product.

## Reframing Heavy Opportunities

When an idea is too broad, search for a smaller wedge:

- Broad CRM -> one report, reminder, importer, template, or daily digest.
- Field service platform -> one niche calculator, quote helper, checklist, or review-response helper.
- Accounting app -> invoice status tracker, tax checklist, receipt naming tool, or CSV cleanup tool.
- Creator management platform -> content production card, hook library, asset readiness checker, or repost planner.
- Health habit app -> symptom log, medication checklist, appointment prep sheet, or printable tracker.
- Travel platform -> group budget poll, itinerary conflict checker, packing checklist, or visa document tracker.

## Preferred Validation Sequence

1. Create a keyword-backed landing page or free mini-tool.
2. Add examples that mirror Reddit/user language.
3. Collect waitlist, email, download, export, or repeat-use signals.
4. Only add accounts, sync, automation, or payment after repeated manual demand is visible.

## Decision Guidance

- `build_now`: V1 can ship in about one month, low-touch acquisition is plausible, and V1 avoids heavy integration/support.
- `prototype`: user pain is real but the interaction must be tested.
- `seo_validate_first`: V1 is simple and useful, but SEO accessibility or search intent must be tested before building the full product.
- `validate_page`: audience demand is plausible but product format, monetization, or channel is premature.
- `observe`: interesting but too broad, too competitive, or unclear distribution.
- `reject`: impossible to validate cheaply, too operationally heavy, or depends on trust/sales the builder does not have.
