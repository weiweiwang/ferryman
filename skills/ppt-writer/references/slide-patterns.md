# Slide Patterns

The portable builder supports these layout families as first-class patterns.
Use them in `deck-spec.json` through `layout` and `layout_family`.

## Cover

- `cover-process`: dark or high-contrast cover with title, subtitle, and a
  three-step process rail.

## Thesis

- `big-claim`: one large claim with 2-4 proof drivers.
- `diagnosis-three`: large claim plus three diagnosis columns.

## Comparison

- `two-column-compare`: two large panels for before/after, option A/B, or two
  customer contexts.
- `matrix`: compact 2x2 or criteria comparison.

## Flow

- `horizontal-flow`: 4-7 sequential steps with arrows.
- `two-phase-plan`: two large phase panels connected by a bridge arrow.

## Evidence

- `metric-rail`: 3-5 large metrics with labels and support notes.
- `table`: compact table with header and row emphasis.
- `ranked-list`: ordered evidence stack.

## Timeline

- `timeline`: milestone or roadmap line with dated entries.

## Text-Led

- `quote`: large quote or customer voice with attribution.
- `section`: chapter divider.
- `generic`: fallback layout; use sparingly.

## Image-Led

- `full-bleed-photo`: one dominant 16:9 image with large overlaid title and
  minimal caption text.
- `photo-caption`: large image plus short explanatory title, suitable for
  classroom or consumer storytelling.
- `image-grid`: 2-4 images with short labels; use for timeline moments,
  places, people, products, or visual comparison.
- `classroom-takeaway`: simple final slide with a large visual, 3-5 keywords,
  and one plain-language takeaway.

Rhythm rule: for decks with 8+ slides, use at least four layout families. For
10+ slides, aim for five or more.
