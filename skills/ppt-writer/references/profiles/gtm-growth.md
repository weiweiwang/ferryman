# gtm-growth

Use for sales, marketing, customer growth, subscription, channel, and go-to-market
decks.

Hard gates:

- Show a visible growth loop, funnel, segment logic, cohort behavior, or channel
  motion.
- Tie tactics to conversion, retention, monetization, or distribution proof.
- Do not rely on vague claims such as "increase awareness" without evidence or
  a measurable mechanism.
- Customer-facing examples should sound usable, not like internal planning
  filler.
