# finance-ir

Use for earnings, investor relations, valuation, operating reviews, and finance
decks.

Hard gates:

- Use exact reported figures, units, dates, and source names.
- Every non-appendix slide needs `sources`.
- Keep bridge logic explicit: revenue, margin, cash, guidance, mix, cohort, or
  sensitivity.
- Do not invent market reactions, deal values, or financial impact to make a
  slide feel complete.
- Footnotes should be quiet but present when figures are shown.
