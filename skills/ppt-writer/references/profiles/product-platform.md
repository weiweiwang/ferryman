# product-platform

Use for SaaS, platform, product strategy, product architecture, and workflow
decks.

Hard gates:

- Show product-to-business linkage: user workflow, adoption signal, monetization
  logic, efficiency impact, or system boundary.
- Prefer architecture maps, workflow diagrams, capability ladders, and proof
  tables over generic feature card grids.
- Avoid slides that could work after swapping only the product name.
- Product screenshots or UI images must be user-provided, verified, or omitted.
