# Publishing Taxonomy

Use this controlled vocabulary for report frontmatter only. Do not invent tags.
Leave uncertain categories empty.

## Rules

- `market`: where the security trades or the listing form.
- `sector`: broad, stable economic sector.
- `industry`: specific business model or profit pool.
- `theme`: investment-relevant trait, risk, or thesis.
- Prefer continuity with prior reports for the same company unless the business
  mix clearly changed.
